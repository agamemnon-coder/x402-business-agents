# Built after the kit

Moved from the [README](../README.md) on 15 November, to keep it short.

<details><summary><b>The payables inbox, scanned invoices, batch approval, the console, the attack rounds, connected accounts</b></summary>

On [/try](https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/try) Claude reads a test company's invoice and the CFO signs the exact terms with a passkey (Face ID on iPhone). The payables inbox below is built to do that for every supplier invoice of a small company. It runs on this site as the [hands-on demo](https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/try) and [/books](https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/books), on test money; the product console is a private preview. Its code is in this repository: the inbox in [src/ap](../src/ap), the books in [src/ledger](../src/ledger), the documents in [src/vault](../src/vault), the accounting links in [src/erp](../src/erp), payments in other currencies in [src/payouts](../src/payouts), and Purser Cloud, the console and its API, in [cloud/](../cloud). It is built and tested against stand-ins and on Cloudflare's local database; on 10 November it ran once end to end in the Airwallex sandbox for three test companies (1 payout PAID, 3 refused by Airwallex: [run record](runs/2026-11-10-platform-e2e.md)). Purser Cloud runs publicly as a test preview at [purser-try.max-31b.workers.dev](https://purser-try.max-31b.workers.dev/), where *Try as guest* opens a demo company read-only (made-up data from a simulated month); what ran live and when is [above](../README.md#whats-live-whats-simulated). The product's own console deployment is another matter: that hosted preview exists but is private (behind an access key); no customer uses it, and it is not ready for real money. Its connected-account payout ran end to end in the Airwallex sandbox on 10 November: EUR 4.84 PAID on a test company's behalf (`593c340b-1752-4537-9ef2-0eb3558897cc`, settlement simulated), with Purser's EUR 0.02 fee, booked in T:0 ([run record](runs/2026-11-10-platform-e2e.md)); an earlier run that morning paid EUR 5.00 with a EUR 0.05 fee (`a9460dba-f31b-4111-be58-6d9a9e71adb1`, [record](runs/2026-11-10-connected-accounts-sandbox.md)). The Kit 11 build keeps Alta and x402 for the agents' own payments; the inbox pays suppliers through Airwallex payouts. Details and screenshots: [docs/after-the-kit.md](after-the-kit.md).

- **Payables inbox:** supplier invoices arrive as PDF or e-mail, are read, checked (duplicates, changed bank details, totals) and booked.
- **Scanned invoices:** two independent readings must agree on IBAN, total, currency and invoice number; otherwise a person decides.
- **One Face ID for a batch:** the CFO signs a list of exact terms once; each payment still runs and is checked on its own.
- **Cloud console** with passkey sign-in, a register of every money movement, Dutch screens.
- **Six red-team rounds:** every confirmed finding fixed with a test, except one point from round 6 that is still open ([docs/go-live.md](go-live.md)).
- **Airwallex connected accounts:** each customer would open its own Airwallex account through Purser; every payout and card call is checked against that company's signed account binding. Live in the sandbox since 10 November: 3 connected accounts ACTIVE; payouts with Purser's fee from the first (above, and an end-to-end run from invoice to T:0); the two newer accounts' payouts refused by Airwallex, which wants the paying company's registration number ([run record](runs/2026-11-10-platform-e2e.md)).
