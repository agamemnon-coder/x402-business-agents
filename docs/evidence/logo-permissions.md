## Alta (formerly Metal), 14 November 2026

Metal renamed itself Alta. Its team in the hackathon's Slack (#metal-kits-11-and-12, 14 Nov 05:09): "We are no longer
called Metal, we are now Alta (alta.finance). From now on, please refer to us by the new name." Metal's old wordmark
(`src/ui/logos/metal.svg`) is no longer shown anywhere a visitor looks; the file stays in the repository, unchanged.

No Alta logo file is used. Read on 14 Nov 2026: alta.finance has no brand or press kit (its pages: home, about, news,
solutions, careers, contact, security, privacy, terms), and its terms (https://alta.finance/terms) say: "“Alta,” the
Alta logos, and related names and marks are trademarks of Alta and may not be used without our prior written
consent." No written consent is on record here, so Alta appears as its name in text, on the same neutral tile as the
other partners (Home's "Works with" row) and named in text in the README's "Built with" line.
