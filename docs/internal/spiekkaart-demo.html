Top-5 Q&A (docs/submission/qa.md)

  1. Why Face ID, not a password? A password is the same for every payment; a passkey signs the hash of these exact terms, so one cent more makes it worthless.
  2. What was live, what simulated? Live: Airwallex sandbox, Metal testnet, 24 of 24 Claude decisions, my passkey (Face ID). Simulated: supplier, customer, merchant, SWIFT SENT/PAID, and the second approver, a labelled test passkey.
  3. Why three models? Honestly, Haiku alone passed our everyday cases too; on a harder dispute it misread one order, so Sonnet reads documents. Opus is the least proven part.
  4. Server compromised? Metal and Airwallex still refuse over the mandate and the card limit. A compromised runner host is out of scope today; keys go to a KMS first.
  5. Can the jury's passkey touch the real CFO? No: the test world and its passkey live in their own store and are enrolled nowhere else.
