# Requirements matrix

This page lists what the Airwallex Agentic Banking Hackathon asks for, where Purser does it, and the proof. It is dated 30 October 2026. The product is Purser ("Give every AI agent its own company card, with limits the CFO sets"). The example company is Northwind Outdoor.

Legend:

- ✅ met, with evidence
- 🟠 partly met, or the evidence is still to come
- ❌ not met

Rules we followed:

- Quotes are literal. Their ids (H1, J5.1, G6, K7a and so on) match our requirements-sources notes.
- † in the Evidence column means the proof comes from the 29 October rehearsals with the earlier example company (Tidemark Data, EUR/USD ticks, Kestrel Quant, Feedstock Labs, Lizenzhaus Beispiel GmbH). The Friday runs of 30 October with Northwind Outdoor ran the same flows (logs `docs/runs/2026-10-30-friday-1.md` and `-2.md`); rows marked † still cite the rehearsal ids.
- "Simulated" means the Airwallex sandbox or our own code simulates that step. We say so in each row where it applies.
- Run logs are in `docs/runs/`. Metal transaction links are in `docs/metal-award.md`.
