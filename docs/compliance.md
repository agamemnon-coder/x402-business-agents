# Compliance: who does what, and how Purser supports it

Purser is not certified, licensed or audited against any of the rules below, and this page does not claim it is.
It shows how the design is ready for them: for each topic, what the rule asks, who is responsible, and what Purser
does, with the proof in this repository. Today Purser runs on test money only: the Airwallex sandbox and Alta (formerly Metal)
testnet.

For each requirement of nine frameworks (the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 Annex A, SOC 2, DORA, PSD2's strong customer
authentication, the GDPR, eIDAS, NIS2 Art. 21 and ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A), the control map
([compliance/controls.md](compliance/controls.md)) names the Purser controls mapped to it, how their evidence is
derived from the ledger, and the gaps. `npm run compliance-evidence` writes, for a period, the records that show each
control ran, with a signed manifest; `npm run verify-compliance-evidence` checks it offline against the signed audit
pack of the same period ([how to run and verify](compliance/README.md)). Per framework, by status (generated from
`src/compliance/controls.ts`):

the [GDPR record and DPIA](compliance/gdpr.md).

Who is who:

- **Airwallex** is the regulated party for the bank side: accounts, card issuing, FX and payouts. Its KYB on the
  business and its screening of payouts are Airwallex's own (not shown in this repo). The agents' cards sit under
  cardholders the business creates; in the sandbox they carry placeholder personal details.
- **Alta** provides the chain, *USDZ and the contracts that enforce mandates, clearing and receive policy.
- **The business** (in the demo, Northwind Outdoor) owns the money, sets the CFO policy and approves.
- **Purser** is software the business runs. It holds no customer money, never sees a card number, and cannot approve
  anything on the CFO's behalf.

## 1. Roles and money

| What the rule asks | Who | How Purser supports it | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Only a licensed party holds and moves client money | Airwallex (bank side), Alta (chain) | The money sits in the business's own Airwallex account and Alta account. Purser asks those rails to move it; card limits at Airwallex and mandate limits on Alta apply whatever Purser asks; FX and SWIFT payouts are controlled by Purser's floors and the CFO's approval. | `docs/airwallex.md`, `docs/metal-award.md` |
| Know your customer and business | Airwallex | Each agent's card has a `DELEGATE` cardholder under the business's account: someone the business lets spend on its behalf. Airwallex's checks apply to the business. | `src/airwallex/issuing.ts` (`createCardholder`) |

## 2. EU AI Act: human oversight, logging, transparency, robustness

| What the rule asks | Who | How Purser supports it | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| A person can oversee the AI and stop it | The business, with Purser's controls | No model output moves money. Alta holds each agent's on-chain budget, Airwallex each card's limit and Purser's code the rest; anything above the CFO's limits waits for the CFO's Face ID on the exact terms (small refunds and task cards within the policy run alone). Optional four eyes above a threshold the CFO sets. One Face ID stops every agent payment and freezes every card (payments stopped live on the Airwallex sandbox on 26 November, [record](runs/2026-11-26-stop-at-rail-live.md); the card freeze on a stop tested against stand-ins). | `src/policy/approvals.ts`, `src/policy/fourEyes.ts`, `src/policy/rules.ts` (`frozen`), `test/freeze.test.ts` |
| Each AI step is logged | Purser | Every Claude reading is an audit row with the model, the request id, a hash of the input, the checked output and the token use; a fallback to the plain parser is logged too, with the reason. | `src/claude/reader.ts` (`claude.read`, `claude.fallback`) |
| People know when AI is involved | Purser | Proposals show which agent proposed and why; the dashboard says the agents are AI and that a person decides; simulated parts are labelled. | `src/dashboard/page.html`, `docs/archive/overview.md` |
| Accuracy and robustness | Purser | Evals of the agents' readings run in CI on the deterministic parsers, and with the Claude models on demand (`npm run evals`); a red team attacks Purser and every attack must fail; injection in documents is caught before money moves. | `src/evals/cases.ts`, `docs/redteam/`, `docs/fraud-and-injection.md`, `test/adversarial.test.ts` |

## 3. PSD2 and strong customer authentication (the idea)

| What the rule asks | Who | How Purser supports it | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two factors to approve a payment, bound to its amount and payee | Airwallex for its own logins; Purser for the CFO's approval | The approval is a passkey on the CFO's phone (something you have) unlocked by Face ID (something you are). The signature covers a hash of the exact terms: amount, payee, fee ceiling, expiry. Change one character and it no longer fits. | `docs/phone-approval.md`, `src/passkey/webauthn.ts`, `test/approve-client.test.ts` |

## 4. GDPR: as little personal data as possible

| What the rule asks | Who | How Purser supports it | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep only the personal data you need | The business, Purser | Agents are cardholders, not people. No card number or CVV ever reaches Purser or a model. The CFO's passkey stores a public key, not biometrics: Face ID stays on the phone. | `docs/cards-and-clearing.md`, `docs/security.md` |
| No personal data in logs or AI context without need | Purser | For model reads the audit log stores a hash of the document, not its text (chat messages and quoted planted lines are stored as text). Secrets are redacted from every text that leaves the runner. No key or credential ever reaches a model (tested). | `src/claude/reader.ts`, `src/lib/redact.ts`, `test/adversarial.test.ts` |
| Retention | The business | Defaults per kind of record on the runner's ledger (the books seven years, to 31 December; short-lived records a day), and `npm run retention -- --check`, which reports what is past its period and deletes nothing; the log is append-only, so expired records leave whole, by a procedure the business runs (not built). Purser Cloud deletes on its own schedule. | [compliance/retention.md](compliance/retention.md), `src/compliance/retention.ts`, [privacy/retention.md](privacy/retention.md) |

## 5. Money laundering, sanctions and fraud

| What the rule asks | Who | How Purser supports it | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Screen payments against sanctions lists | Airwallex; on chain, Purser's send policy | Euro payments go to beneficiaries Airwallex validates (and, in production, screens). On Alta, funds held in Purser's send-policy contract leave only with a decision Purser signed after screening the payee; without it the chain reverts. Live on testnet in a test world with a test list and a stand-in sanctioned address (30 October); not used in the recorded run, and agents' mandate payments do not pass through it. | `docs/airwallex.md`, `docs/runs/2026-10-30-compliance-policy.md` |
| Pay only known payees | Purser, on chain by Alta | An Alta mandate names its one payee. A supplier's changed bank details count only after a request, a person's check outside the system and the CFO's approval, which shows the full new IBAN. | `src/policy/payeeChange.ts`, `test/payee-change.test.ts` |
| Stop invoice fraud | Purser | The fraud check blocks changed bank details; a model's reading of an invoice must agree with the invoice's own lines on amount, vendor and bank details; planted "pre-approved" lines and hidden characters are flagged and change nothing. | `docs/fraud-and-injection.md` |
| Refuse money from unknown senders | Alta enforces; Purser sets it | Purser can set the sweep wallet to accept test USD only from the business account; Alta then holds anything else in quarantine, and the CFO returns or accepts it with Face ID (live on testnet in a separate world, 30 October; not set in the recorded run). | `src/policy/quarantine.ts`, `docs/metal-award.md`, section 5 |

## 6. Segregation of duties (in the spirit of SOX)

| What the rule asks | Who | How Purser supports it | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| The person who proposes cannot approve | Purser | Agents propose; only the CFO's passkey approves; an agent has no way to sign. With four eyes, two different people sign and the proposer never counts. | `src/policy/approvals.ts`, `test/approvals.test.ts` |
| Changes to the rules are controlled too | Purser | A change to the CFO policy is itself a proposal approved with Face ID; from the phone, limits can only be tightened. | `src/executors/policyChange.ts`, `docs/cfo-policy.md` |
| Two people for the account's own money, on chain | Alta enforces | An Alta account with two owners, the CFO and the controller, and a threshold of two: binding or revoking a mandate, or paying from the account itself, needs both signatures (live on testnet in a test world, 30 October, with two test keys on one machine, not two people; Northwind's recorded account has one owner). | `docs/runs/2026-10-30-four-eyes.md` |

## 7. Record keeping and audit

| What the rule asks | Who | How Purser supports it | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complete, tamper-evident records | Purser | Every payment, decline, refusal and approval is in an append-only, hash-chained audit log; the database refuses edits, deletes and replaced rows through the application, and a change made around it (for example by removing the guard) is found by the next payment, which verifies the chain first and is refused (since 19 Nov), and by `npm run verify`. | `src/db/schema.sql` (`audit_log` triggers), `src/audit/intact.ts`, `npm run verify-log` |
| Records an auditor can check | Purser | `npm run audit:report` writes a PDF; `npm run verify` re-checks every credential, pull, disputed payment, clearing receipt, Face ID approval and Airwallex booking; each payment carries a GL account and cost centre and exports to CSV or JSON. | `docs/audit/audit-report-rehearsal-3.pdf`, `docs/metal-award.md` (section 6, evidence model) |
| Reconciliation | Purser | The runner reconciles the ledger with Airwallex and the chain every hour (and `npm run reconcile` on demand). | `src/finance/reconcile.ts` |
| Finality kept with the record | Purser, from Alta | The runner stores Alta's finality certificate for each chain transaction (what Alta's RPC reported; the validators' signature is not verified) and checks it again in `npm run verify`. | `src/metal/finality.ts` |
| Records a bank's tools can read | Purser | A paid invoice exports as ISO 20022 pacs.008, linking the Airwallex SWIFT transfer and the Alta transaction, valid against the official schema. | `docs/iso20022/pacs008-BTX-2026-11.xml` |

## 8. Operational resilience (in the spirit of DORA)

| What the rule asks | Who | How Purser supports it | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contain an incident fast | The business, with Purser's controls | The emergency stop: one Face ID makes Purser refuse to sign any agent payment and freezes every card at Airwallex (payments stopped live on the Airwallex sandbox on 26 November, [record](runs/2026-11-26-stop-at-rail-live.md); the card freeze on a stop tested against stand-ins). The Alta mandates stay bound until revoked. | `test/freeze.test.ts` |
| Survive an outside service failing | Purser | Timeouts and retries per service; a payment without an answer is looked up, never resent; without a model the plain parsers take over; without keys the demo replays the recorded run. | `docs/security.md` (timeouts table), `docs/run-it.md` |
| Know what happened | Purser | The hardening log lists each finding and its fix. | `docs/hardening.md` |
| Know what to do | The business | A runbook for incidents, the emergency stop, key rotation and recovery, saying which steps were exercised live. | `docs/runbook.md`, `docs/controls-matrix.md` |

## 9. Card rules

| What the rule asks | Who | How Purser supports it | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Limits and allowed merchants per card | Airwallex enforces; the CFO sets | Each agent's card has a per-payment and monthly limit and allowed merchant categories (software 5734, helpdesk software 7372), synced from the CFO policy and read back; Airwallex declines the rest. Cards end after 90 days. | `src/config/policy.ts` (`CARDS`, `PLANS`, `CARD_ACTIVE_DAYS`), `src/policy/cards.ts`, `test/cards.test.ts` |
| No card numbers where they are not needed | Purser | Agents and models never see the card number or CVV. | `docs/security.md` |

## What a real launch would add

- A retention policy and a data processing agreement with each provider.
- Named people for the incident procedure ([runbook.md](runbook.md) has the procedure).
- Sanctions screening on every on-chain payout from the business account: the send policy ran in a test world
  (`contracts/ComplianceSendPolicy.sol`), but agents' mandate payments do not pass through it, and it is not in the
  recorded run.
- An outside audit of the controls above.
