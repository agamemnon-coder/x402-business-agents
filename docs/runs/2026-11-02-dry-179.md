## Root causes

1. **Item 5: Sourcing's own policy maximum sat at the mandate cap.** The runner and agents start before `world serve`,
   per runbook "Vooraf" 7 and then step 1.
   - At 21:53:22 Sourcing found both suppliers unreachable and set the buy policy to `none` (seq 22). Query #1 was
     therefore turned away.
   - After `serve` and Ridgeline's notice, Sourcing set `{supplier: stockline, max_price: 0.03, cache 0}` at 21:56:03
     (seq 46, request `req_011Cger7vUnVPwPRqUVbty6U`). 0.03 is exactly its mandate cap.
   - The 0.04 attempt was therefore refused by the agent's own policy, before Purser's mandate check. No
     `mandate_cap_exceeded` and no on-chain record followed.
   - At 22:01:09 Sourcing set the policy to `none` (seq 113), so the armed let-one-through never met an over-cap purchase.
     It lapsed.
   - The runbook names this case under "Eerlijk erbij zeggen". The acceptance still requires item 5.
   - Fix (agent M, `fix-dry179`): Purser's mandate-cap pre-check runs before the agent's own buy-policy maximum, and an
     armed let-one-through is not stopped by the policy maximum.
2. **The replace failed because of Treasury's own sweep.**
   - Treasury created P-0002 and P-0003 at 22:01:37 (seq 133–134), with `spent_at_proposal` 0.00 for its own step.
   - On its next wake (the `customers.turned_away` event), Treasury called `sweep_earnings`: 0.45 from its mandate at
     22:01:47 (seq 149).
   - After both passkeys (22:02:37), `src/executors/mandateReplace.ts` refused the replace at 22:02:39: "spent moved from
     0.00 to 0.45 since the proposal; a new one is needed".
   - Fix (agent M): no spending from a mandate while its replace proposal is open.

    Sourcing stayed blocked (policy `none`) and turned 53 customers away for the rest of the run.
  - The phone said "Approved, but execution stopped", with no reason.
- **Start order (the holdouts' point a).** The runbook starts the runner and agents ("Vooraf" 7) before `world serve`
  (step 1). The agents' first routine checks therefore find no suppliers.
  - Sourcing set `none`; Treasury and Sourcing said "unreachable".
  - Query #1 was refused; only 15 sales up to #16.
  - The runbook is ambiguous here. For the next run the driver starts `world serve` and checks that Stockline answers
    before the runner starts. That is a change of the runbook's order; it needs a line in the runbook.
- **Sweep timing.** Treasury swept on the turned-away wake, before `world money-in`. `money-in` then reported "net
  earnings 0.00" and Treasury had nothing to do. The fix for root cause 2 changes this too.
- **Treasury's licence advice had a sum wrong** (see the factual-errors row). Code's plan was right: EUR 110.00, which
  is the licence 240.00 plus the fee ceiling 20.00, minus the free 150.00.
- **The card purchase is still PENDING at Airwallex.** `card_transactions` holds status PENDING with `approved` 1, so it
  is an authorisation, not yet settled. That is expected in the sandbox.
