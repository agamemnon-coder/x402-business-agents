## What the check does in the product

Purser's month close now has an independent ledger check (`closeMonthWhenT0Agrees` in `src/ledger/close.ts`): the
month is locked only when T:0's read-back agrees with Purser's books. With Purser's own entries at hand it compares
entry by entry and line by line; a one-cent difference refuses the close and names the entry
(`test/close-gate.test.ts`). /t0 and Purser Cloud's Books screen show the result above, computed from the record at
build, with the read-back to download.

The entry-by-entry comparison needs the main run's ledger, which is not in the repository; this record's check is
against the books' recorded totals per account. On 10 Nov the comparison against that ledger found the same 95
entries and 0 differences.

Made with `npm run t0:readback -- --from <saved response>`; without `--from` the script makes the one GET itself.
