## Why it matters to a CFO

The CFO signs one list. Airwallex holds exactly that list as one object, carrying the signed hash, and Purser refuses to
submit unless what Airwallex would book is, item by item and to the cent, what was signed. Airwallex's approval workflow
then applies to the batch as a whole: a second, independent lock.
