Max gave the go for a new webhook subscription ("Go webhooks"), which means a new signing secret.

## What ran

1. `npm run cards -- webhook --replace`, with the events in `ALL_EVENTS` (`src/policy/airwallexEvents.ts`).
   - Airwallex refused the first attempt: `401 Insufficient permissions for subscribing to conversion.overdue`. The
     create call failed, so nothing was made and the old subscription stayed.
   - The second attempt, without `conversion.overdue`, made `wh_Wnhwa…` with **23 events** (API version 2025-11-11).
     The new secret went to the Worker (`wrangler secret put`, by stdin) and to the runner's keys dir; then the old
     subscription `wh_CJ4ts…` (4 card transaction events) was deleted. No secret was printed.
   - Subscribed: the 4 card transaction events; `issuing.card.active`, `.inactive`, `.closed`, `.blocked`, `.lost`,
     `.stolen`; `payout.transfer.scheduled`, `.processing`, `.sent`, `.paid`, `.failed`, `.cancelled`;
     `conversion.scheduled`, `.settled`, `.cancelled`; `deposit.pending`, `.settled`, `.rejected`, `.reversed`.
   - Not subscribed: `conversion.overdue` (this key lacks the permission).
2. The runner (`npm run relay`) on a copy of the recorded ledger with the receivables (`data/hooks-1.sqlite`; not the
   recording).
3. Two new retailer invoices and their payments, simulated by the sandbox, **with no poll**, so any match had to come
   from the webhook: NW-R-201 (EUR 300.00) and NW-R-202 (GBP 120.00, paid 100.00).
4. One card purchase inside the Operations agent's limits: Packwise, EUR 19.99, approved by Airwallex.
