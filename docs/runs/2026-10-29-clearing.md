# Run log: Metal clearing for data sales, 2026-10-29

Metal's Offer Pay-per-Call Clearing (x402 scheme `bajillion`) went live on testnet in October 2026
([docs](https://docs.metalntwx.com/agents/clearing)). Our installed `@metalntwx/metal-pay` 0.3.4 already has it.
Everything below ran on Metal testnet, on the rehearsal-3 world. Times are UTC.

## Probe (16:39 to 16:47)

1. **A deposit key for Kestrel.** A new EOA was funded from the faucet with 100 *USDZ (tx `0xcd0f125f…`). Its key is
   in the keys repo.
2. **Clearing wallets.** Each side got one (Kestrel and our seller): a P-256 clearing key and a receipt journal.
3. **Deposit.** Kestrel deposited 5.00 *USDZ (approve `0x1f80eb68…`, deposit
   [`0x055ba899…6766`](https://testnet.metalntwx.com/tx/0x055ba89987f658cb038fcbd1660f4d9e98e78e4c44c86c5346946f8cca1a6766)).
   It was spendable about 10 seconds later.
4. **Payments.** A probe seller offered `exact` and `bajillion` side by side, at 0.07. Kestrel paid by clearing:
   - each payment came back with a receipt (payer and operator signatures), status `preconfirmed`;
   - there was no transaction per query;
   - each took 2 to 10 seconds, including the retries described next.
5. **Between epochs.** The service often refused the first try with `between_epochs` (a pause of a few seconds at
   each epoch close; nothing is paid). The next try 3 seconds later went through. The agent now retries this, up to
   8 times.
6. **The important finding.** Kestrel is charged even when the seller refuses.
   - The probe seller refused one payment in its `onBeforeSettle` hook (as if the supplier had refused). Kestrel was
     charged anyway.
   - After 5 clearing payments and 4 deliveries, Kestrel's spendable balance was 4.65 and ours was 0.35.
   - So a clearing payment is counted when the payer makes it, before the seller can say no.
   - **Consequence for the design:** the seller may offer clearing only when it is sure it can deliver.

## The design that follows

- **Two routes.**
  - `/v1/fx-tick` (exact, under Kestrel's mandate, with its signed order) is unchanged. S1 and S2 work as before;
    nothing moves unless the tick is delivered.
  - `/v1/fx-tick/prepaid` takes clearing only.
- **A free check before the offer.** Before the prepaid route answers 402, the sourcing agent looks at the
  supplier's own 402 offer (free, nothing is signed). It compares the price with the sourcing mandate's cap and the
  CFO policy's limit. If the purchase would be refused, the prepaid route answers 503 "not offered" before any offer,
  so nobody pays.
- **Fallback.**
  - Kestrel tries the prepaid route first. If clearing took nothing (not offered, the service unavailable, not
    enough deposit, or still between epochs after the retries), Kestrel buys on the mandate route instead.
  - If a clearing payment may have been counted (a lost answer, a held payment), it never pays the same tick a
    second way.
- **Recording.** A clearing sale is recorded with its receipt (`sales.scheme = 'bajillion'`, `clearing_json`).
- **Dashboard.** It shows the prepaid budget apart from the mandates.

## Live check of the fallback (16:48)

- The CFO policy limits the sourcing agent to 0.03, and the supplier asks 0.04.
- `npm run clearing -- buy 53 53`:
  - the prepaid route answered 503 "not offered" (`policy_cap_exceeded`);
  - Kestrel fell back to the mandate route;
  - there the sourcing purchase was refused before signing, and Kestrel was not charged.
