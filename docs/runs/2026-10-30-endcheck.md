### What the runs found, and what changed

1. **A phone can hold another run's runner key.** In eind-1 Max could not approve P-0002 and approved P-0001 instead; in eind-2 the
   same link opened in WhatsApp's own browser showed "What the relay sent is not signed by the runner this phone knows" (that browser
   still held eind-1's key). The check did its job: nothing was signed for a runner the phone does not know. Opened in a private Safari
   tab, P-0002 went through at once. Every link for Max now says: copy it, open Safari, private tab.
2. **An invoice proposal expired while the CFO was busy with another.** The FX quote is locked for minutes, the proposal for ten, and
   it was made while Max decided the dispute. `npm run demo:all` now asks for a fresh quote and a new proposal when one expires (at most
   three times; a rejection still stops it). Commit `faf9853`.
3. **Twice a refund Opus advised rejecting was approved by a CFO who meant to reject** (run 2 on Friday, and eind-2). The signature was
   valid and over the right hash, so no check failed: the mistake was the tap. When Opus advises rejecting, the phone's summary now
   says "Approve anyway with Face ID", arms only after 3 seconds, and leads with a wide "Reject instead". The phone browser check covers
   it. Commit `92daca0`.
