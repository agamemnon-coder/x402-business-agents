# The public /try, a mega run: 50 walks one after another, 19 and 20 November 2026

The judge's journey on the public demo, https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/try, run end to end again and again as a
visitor, one walk at a time, with every answer the page got kept: in Chromium at 1440 px light, 390 px dark (a phone)
and 768 px (a tablet, light and dark), and in Firefox at 1440 light and 390 dark. The same walk as the 8 of the
morning of 19 Nov ([2026-11-19-live-try.md](2026-11-19-live-try.md)). **Test money only**: each walk is a fresh /try
test company on the simulated bank, its payouts go to the **Airwallex sandbox**, where PAID is reached through the
sandbox's own simulation endpoint. Nothing was deployed, no admin route was called and no key of ours was used.

**50 walks over two UTC days: 17 on 19 Nov, 33 on 20 Nov, resumed by plan.** On 19 Nov the series stopped itself after
walk 17 on its own rule (3 walks in a row marked failed), and it would have had to stop one walk later anyway: the
deployed /try makes at most **100 Airwallex sandbox payouts a UTC day for all its visitors** (`AWX_PAYOUT_DAY` in
`worker/src/awxPayout.ts`, 2 per walk), and our own walks had used 58 of them that day. We agreed to leave at least 40
of the day's payouts to other visitors and judges, so the remaining 33 walks were left for another UTC day. They ran on
20 Nov, from walk 18 where the series had stopped (see [Resuming](#resuming)): **66 of that day's 100 payouts, leaving
34 for visitors.**

**Result: every step the site was asked to do passed in 49 of 50 walks; the recording script marked 46 of 50 "all
ok", and all 33 of 20 Nov.** The 4 it did not are all from 19 Nov: walk 2 was stopped by a bug in the script itself,
after the proof download; walks 15, 16 and 17 passed every site step, and were marked failed only because /verify,
redeployed during the series, now says "Proof valid · payment refused" for a refusal's valid proof, words the script
did not know yet (it took them in every walk of 20 Nov). 100 Airwallex sandbox transfers, each SCHEDULED then PAID (50
× EUR 120.00, 50 × EUR 1,850.00, 100 distinct ids); 50 approvals whose hash is the one the 1,850.00 transfer carries;
50 one-cent changes refused with no transfer; 49 EUR 480.00 invoices held by code with no payout (walk 2 did not reach
it); 100 of 100 proof files VALID with `npm run verify-bundle`, 98 of them also VALID on /verify (walk 2's were not
opened there). 735 Claude entries (1,136 requests to the API, 1,024 distinct request ids) cost USD 0.241903, all by
`claude-haiku-5-5`, **none fell back to the parser**. No 5xx; one 429, on 19 Nov, from the script's own reading of the
engine's events, answered by a 60 s back-off; none on 20 Nov. No console error.

## What each walk did

The walk of [2026-11-19-live-try.md](2026-11-19-live-try.md#what-each-run-did), unchanged: launch; EUR 120.00 read,
checked and paid by the agent alone, simulated to PAID in the Airwallex sandbox; EUR 1,850.00 sent to the CFO, a passkey
made and the exact terms signed, paid, simulated to PAID; the one-cent change (1,850.01) with the earlier signature,
refused; the proof downloaded and checked on /verify (and the paid proof pasted there), both also with the repository's
verifier; in a second tab, a test company of its own, the EUR 480.00 invoice that code's cash check holds. Every walk
also checked that no request left the site's host and that the page logged no console or page error.

Recorded by [`scripts/try-live-record.ts`](../../scripts/try-live-record.ts), one process per walk, driven by
[`scripts/try-live-series.ts`](../../scripts/try-live-series.ts):

- **One at a time.** A walk starts only when the one before has ended and at least 35 s have passed (and at least 30 s
  after the previous start). Measured on 19 Nov: 35.0 to 95.1 s from one walk's end to the next one's start, 90.1 to
  160.8 s start to start; on 20 Nov: 35.0 s every time end to start, 96.3 to 138.3 s start to start. Between walk 17's
  end (08:53:11, 19 Nov) and walk 18's start (03:55:31, 20 Nov): 19 h 2 min.
- **Backing off.** After a walk that saw a 429 or a 5xx from the site, the next waits 60 s more (120 s if it happens
  again in a row). It happened once in the 50: walk 1's 429, so walk 2 waited 95 s.
- **Stopping.** After 3 walks in a row that failed or in which Claude fell back, or on a full disk. It stopped the
  series once, after walk 17; on 20 Nov it did not, and the series ended at walk 50 as planned.
- **The passkey.** Chromium: its virtual authenticator over CDP (ctap2, internal, user verification on and verified),
  as on the morning of 19 Nov. Firefox has no virtual authenticator under Playwright, so the repository's software
  passkey ([`test/browser/support/softPasskey.ts`](../../test/browser/support/softPasskey.ts), test only) is put in
  every page before it loads: a real ES256 key made with WebCrypto, whose registration and signatures the server checks
  as it does a phone's. It needed one addition to sign on /try: its credentials now answer
  `getClientExtensionResults()` (with `{}`), which /try reads for every passkey. Not a person's Face ID in either
  browser.
- **Firefox's trust store.** Outbound HTTPS from this machine goes through a proxy with its own certificate authority,
  which Chromium trusts from the machine's NSS store (`~/.pki/nssdb`) and Firefox does not (its profile has its own).
  With `NSSDB=<dir>` the script starts Firefox on a fresh profile whose certificate store is a copy of that NSS store.
  No certificate check is turned off.
- **Planned order** (50): walks 5, 13, 21, 29, 37 and 45 at 768 (light, then dark, alternating); 9, 17, 25, 33, 41 and
  49 in Firefox (1440 light, then 390 dark, alternating); the rest alternating Chromium 1440 light and 390 dark. All 50
  ran in that order: Chromium 1440 light 19 (7 on 19 Nov, 12 on 20 Nov), Chromium 390 dark 19 (6, 13), Chromium 768
  light 3 (walks 5, 21, 37), Chromium 768 dark 3 (13, 29, 45), Firefox 1440 light 3 (9, 25, 41), Firefox 390 dark 3
  (17, 33, 49).
