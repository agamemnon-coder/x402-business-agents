   - It was stopped by its process group after #1 and #2. Both were sold normally at 0.05 with the default policy, and
     no agent had started yet.
   - Serve was restarted with the right environment before the 402 check. From then on the order was exact.

   money-in Treasury found 0.00 and moved nothing.
3. **Query #37 was refused `mandate_revoked` while the replace swapped the sourcing mandate.** The customer got 503 and
   nothing moved.
4. **Cosmetic issues:**
   - `world let-one-through` printed "not let through (in_flight)" when its wait ended while the pull was in flight.
     The ledger's state is `used / metal_refused`.
   - The replace's confirm sheet says "The sourcing mandate may pay an address the ledger has no name for: 0x8d8bef…"
     (Ridgeline's payee address is not named).
5. **Treasury's routine wake after money-in** mentioned that the `mandate_cap_exceeded` flag was still visible under
   the new 0.04 cap, and asked Sourcing whether anything was still refused. It proposed nothing.
