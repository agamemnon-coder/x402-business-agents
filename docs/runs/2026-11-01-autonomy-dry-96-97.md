# Autonomy world runs dry-96 and dry-97 (1 November)

A fresh test world each time (`data/dry-N.sqlite`, its own keys): Metal testnet, the Airwallex sandbox, real Claude.
Flags: `KIT11_AGENTS=on KIT11_AUTONOMY=on`, routine check every 5 minutes. `DRYRUN_AUTONOMY=1 npx tsx test/browser/dryrun.check.ts`.
We only change the world (`npm run world -- serve | price-rise | dispute | invoice`). The agents decide what to do. The CFO and the
controller sign with their (virtual) passkeys.

  (the cache seconds field). Fixed in 3f7f769: the schema sent to Claude has no number bounds, and zod still checks every input.
- **Support's settlement and `verify` stopped.** Summit's email names orders #5–#16, but in a live world #4 and later numbers
  were never sold (the shop stopped selling after the price rise). Fixed: a named number with no delivered sale is listed and
  gets nothing refunded (`salesFound`). A number sold twice still fails.
- Also fixed: the agents woke before bootstrap and logged a failure every 3 s (they now wait quietly). The dry run matched the
  wrong Treasury decision to the invoice (it now matches each decision to its world event). It waits for 16 stock checks
  before the dispute.
- What went right in dry-96: Treasury (Opus) told Sourcing to switch to Ridgeline instead of asking the CFO for a rebalance.
  Support refunded nothing without verified orders and flagged the planted address in the email.
