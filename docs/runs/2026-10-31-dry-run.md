## Found, and fixed

- The facts for the draft reply to Summit said "wrong prices" while the supplier's correction was about stock levels;
  Sonnet repeated it. `src/cli/s2.ts` now says "wrong stock levels".
- The receive policy is now set inside `npm run demo:all`, before the sweep, so the sweep always lands on a wallet that
  accepts the business account alone.
- Four eyes now covers every change of the agents' mandates (Kit 11's rebalance), at any amount, once the CFO turns it on.
