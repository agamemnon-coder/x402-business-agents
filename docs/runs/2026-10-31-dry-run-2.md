# Second dry run: the whole draaiboek with two passkeys (31 October)

`test/browser/dryrun.check.ts` runs every step of the [draaiboek](../internal/draaiboek-live-run.md) in a throwaway world
(`data/dry-N.sqlite`, keys in `…-keys/dry-N`). It runs live on Metal testnet and in the Airwallex sandbox, with
test money, the real runner and executors, and a local relay. Two Chromium phones play the people: `test-cfo` and
`test-controller`. Each has its own virtual authenticator, so these are two passkeys and two people. These are test
identities, never Max or a real second approver, and the world of the live run was not touched. The script refuses any ledger
that is not a new `dry-N`.

Six worlds were needed (dry-6, dry-10, dry-11, dry-12, plus the short dry-8/9/13 checks). Each failure was traced
to its cause, got a test, was fixed, and was run again.

## What the dry runs found, and the fix for each

| Found in | What went wrong | Cause | Fix | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dry-6 | The mandate change and Bergtex were approved after **one** passkey | Turning on four eyes is a policy change, and it waits for the CFO's Face ID; the draaiboek said "Claude zet vier ogen aan" | The draaiboek now has Max approve P-0001 and Claude check `npm run policy`; the dry run approves it and checks it is in force | `dryrun.check.ts` ("four eyes is on … before any mandate change") |
| dry-6 | "Reject instead" on "refund all 12" could not be tapped | The planted `0x000…dEaD` address in the warning does not wrap, so the sheet grew wider than the phone | Warnings and sheet text wrap (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`) | `approve.check.ts` plants that line and checks that nothing runs past 390 px |
| dry-6 | After a runner restart right after Face ID, the licence stayed APPROVED and was never paid | On start the runner resumed only EXECUTING actions | It also starts APPROVED actions, through the executor's own checks (hash, approval, expiry, quote); four eyes is unaffected | `test/resume-approved.test.ts` |
| dry-7/8 | The phone said "P-0001 is not waiting" | A proposal reaches the relay at the runner's next sync, a few seconds later | The approval page looks again for about 20 s | dry-9/13 enrol step |
| dry-10 | After the restart, the phones could not reach the relay | `--local` made a new relay token on each start (the hosted relay keeps its token, so the live run was never affected) | `KIT11_LOCAL_RELAY_TOKEN` keeps one token; it is redacted like the hosted one | `test/redact.test.ts` |
| dry-11 | The bank change was approved by two people, then refused by the executor | A GB account; this build adds German accounts at Airwallex only | The request refuses a non-DE account at once, before anyone is asked | `test/payee-change.test.ts` |
| dry-6 | The "network away" test cut the network before the page loaded | Test order | The network now drops after the sheet is armed | `dryrun.check.ts` |

## Dry-12: every step, in the order of tomorrow

Every step passed. The only failure was the pinned-runner check, in its old form, which looked at the dashboard.
In its new form it looks at the approval page, which refuses to sign; it passed in dry-13. `npm run verify` passed
every check:
- the audit chain;
- every mandate;
- every pull;
- 12 of 12 dispute orders;
- every passkey approval, including the two-person ones;
- the policy versions;
- the finality certificates;
- the Airwallex conversions and transfers, PAID, with the approval hash in their metadata.

The run also showed:
- **Nothing twice.** One transfer per invoice, one decision per proposal, no page errors.
- **Expiry.**
  - Both phones showed the same countdown (9m 47s for 588 s of terms).
  - Approving after expiry signed nothing.
  - A fresh proposal came with a fresh quote.
- **Mis-tap.**
  - Tapping option A by mistake showed its summary; Back closed it and nothing was signed.
  - "Refund all 12" showed the red warning, "You are approving" and "Approve anyway", armed after 3 s.
  - "Reject instead" turned the sheet into a rejection.
- **Recovery.**
  - With the network gone, nothing was approved.
  - With Face ID failed (the phone locked), nothing was approved.
  - The runner was killed right after the licence approval, and after the restart it was paid exactly once.
  - The audit chain stayed whole.
- **Links.**
  - The viewer link opened twice in a fresh private context with no key conflict.
  - A phone that had pinned another runner signs nothing and is told to use a private tab.

Times per step are in the draaiboek. The run took about 13 minutes of machine time, plus an 11-minute wait for the
expiry test.
