## dry-125: what went wrong

1. After Stockline's price rose to 0.04 (over the Sourcing agent's 0.03 cap), the Sourcing agent stopped buying
   (cache only) instead of trying and being refused. Code only let Treasury design a rebalance after "a purchase
   refused over a cap", which never came. Treasury even asked Sourcing to try a 0.04 buy so it would be refused; both
   agents then agreed that would be staging a breach and withdrew it (good). Result: the shop turned every customer
   away for 12 minutes, only 5 stock checks were sold, and Summit's dispute (#5 to #16) was about sales that mostly
   never happened.
2. The dispute script then crashed: of the disputed sales only #5 existed, none had wrong data, and routing an empty
   refund batch is an error by design. The customer's "refund all" never reached the CFO.

Fix (with tests; a fresh review runs on it): code's evidence that an agent is blocked by its mandate now also counts
customers turned away (at least 3 in 15 minutes) while no supplier sells fresh data (at most 5 minutes old) within
the cap; the words for the CFO never say a purchase was refused when none was. A dispute where no delivered sale is
wrong now refunds nothing and still sends the customer's request to the CFO.
