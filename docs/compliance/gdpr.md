## Who is controller

| Party | Role | For |
|---|---|---|
| The deploying business | **Controller** | Its suppliers', customers' and staff's data in the runner and in Purser Cloud |
| Purser Cloud's operator | **Processor** (Art. 28), on the business's instructions | The data Purser Cloud stores; a data processing agreement template exists ([templates/dpa.md](templates/dpa.md)), signed with nobody ([controls.md](controls.md)) |
| The providers of the [register](third-parties.md) | Subprocessors | What each receives, as the register says |

## DPIA for the agents (Art. 35)

**Is one required?** Art. 35(3) does not list Purser's processing: it makes no evaluation of people with legal or
similar effects, no large-scale special-category processing and no systematic monitoring of a public area. It is
done anyway because the agents use new technology (an AI model reads documents that hold personal data), one of the
criteria supervisory authorities use. Done by the maintainer; no data protection officer or data subject consulted.

| Step | For the agents (Sourcing, Support, Treasury; the payables Checker, Coder and Payer) |
|---|---|
| **Description** | The agents read invoices and messages (which can hold a sole trader's name, address and bank details, or a customer's name), propose payments, refunds and rebalances, and write advice. A model reads the text; code checks it; a person approves above the limits. |
| **Necessity and proportionality** | Reading an invoice needs its payee and bank details; nothing else about a person is collected. The model receives the document's text, not the business's other records; no card number or secret ever reaches it. For model readings the audit log keeps a hash of the document, not its text. |
| **Risks to people** | (1) A supplier's bank details changed by a fraudster (BEC), and the supplier not paid. (2) A customer wrongly refused a refund. (3) Personal data sent to a model provider in the United States. (4) A person's data kept longer than needed. (5) A decision about a person by a model. |
| **Measures** | (1) Changed bank details are held and need a check outside the system and the CFO's approval (PC-13). (2) The Support agent cannot refuse: it refunds within the cap or escalates to a person (PC-11). (3) A transfer to Anthropic: a gap until a transfer mechanism is in place (below). (4) [retention.md](retention.md); Purser Cloud erases a person at once. (5) No decision about a person is made by a model (Art. 22 row in [controls.md](controls.md)). |
| **Residual risk** | Medium for (3) until a transfer mechanism is recorded; low for the others ([ai-impact-assessment.md](ai-impact-assessment.md)). |

## Data subjects' rights

| Right | How it is met | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Access (Art. 15) | A user reads their own account: `GET /v1/me` (and its linked identities); an owner exports the company's data as a staged export with single-use links (`POST /v1/export/staged`); a supplier or customer who is not a user is answered by the controller from that export | `cloud/src/app.ts`, [cloud/src/exports.ts](../../cloud/src/exports.ts) |
| Rectification (Art. 16) | A person's language and linked accounts by `/v1/me`; a supplier's bank details only by the payee-change procedure (a check outside the system and the CFO's approval), so a correction cannot be a fraud | `cloud/src/app.ts`, [src/policy/payeeChange.ts](../../src/policy/payeeChange.ts) |
| Erasure (Art. 17) | `POST /v1/users/{id}/erase`: name replaced by a pseudonym, e-mail, passkeys, linked accounts and keys removed at once; the books kept (Art. 17(3)(b)); a company leaves by `POST /v1/offboarding` | [cloud/src/privacy.ts](../../cloud/src/privacy.ts), [docs/privacy/retention.md](../privacy/retention.md#erasing-one-person) |
| Portability (Art. 20) | The company's export, JSON and CSV (`GET /v1/export/csv/{format}/{kind}`) | [cloud/src/exports.ts](../../cloud/src/exports.ts) |
| Not subject to automated decisions (Art. 22) | No decision about a person is made by a model; code pays a supplier's invoice alone only within the limit the CFO signed | [ai-policy.md](ai-policy.md) |

The runner's own ledger has no erasure tool: its hash chains are kept or deleted whole ([retention.md](retention.md#how-records-leave)).

## Transfers outside the EU

Per recipient, from the locations the [third-party register](third-parties.md) records (the table is in the record
above). Anthropic, Resend and Twilio are in the United States. **No transfer mechanism (the EU-US Data Privacy
Framework, Standard Contractual Clauses) is recorded for any of them**: GDPR Art. 44 is a gap in
[controls.md](controls.md) until the controller puts one in place. Which mechanism each recipient would need is in
[transfers.md](transfers.md).

## Breaches

A personal data breach is an incident: [incident-response.md](incident-response.md). The controller notifies the
supervisory authority within 72 hours where feasible (Art. 33) and the people affected when the risk to them is high
(Art. 34). Nothing has been notified: test data only.
