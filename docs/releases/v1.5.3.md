## Purser v1.5.3

**AI agents pay supplier invoices within the limits you set. Above a limit, the CFO signs the exact terms with a passkey (Face ID), and one changed cent breaks the signature.**

Test money only: the Airwallex sandbox and the Alta (formerly Metal) testnet. No real money moves; what is simulated is labelled in each record.

## A red-team release

Two red teams attacked v1.5.2: one against Purser Cloud (sign-in, sessions, guests, tenant isolation, sync, web security, privacy) and one against the runner's money paths. An independent code review read every change since v1.5.0. Every confirmed finding below is fixed with a test that failed first; a second red-team round then attacked the fixes.

### Money moved further than the CFO allowed: fixed
- **A bill split into parts under the limit** was paid alone when the supplier backdated the parts. What Purser approves alone is now totalled per payee over the split window, measured on Purser's own clock, never on dates a supplier prints.
- **A negotiated bill could be paid twice** (as issued and as agreed). One bill is now paid once, by either number, enforced in code and by the database.
- **Four eyes and the second key now cover currency conversions**, valued in euros.
- **A reserve floor could be lowered in steps** to stay under segregation of duties. A lowering is now weighed against the floor's last two-person value.
- **A replacement payout** now runs the same execution guard as every other payout, and only within 7 days of the failure.
- **The emergency stop** now also holds an approved task card and the return of quarantined money; the cash check covers every payment an agent would pay alone.

### Fixed after an independent review
- **The emergency stop in Purser Cloud** was refused for some display names (for example one with a part in brackets). Pulling the stop is never refused for a name.
- **The runner's Books** subtracted receivables payments from closing cash.
- **Payment references** in the SWIFT form `/INV/<number>` match their invoice again; a reference naming two invoices goes to review; a currency in lower case is read.
- **The console's day** goes back to today when you leave the demo, and the Agents page shows a paused agent as paused.

### Privacy
- **No customer or payer name, e-mail or IBAN reaches Purser Cloud** through the ledger, the Transactions register, audit rows or the Cash & FX and Receivables snapshot; a test scans every sync for them, and the cloud refuses a snapshot carrying an IBAN or e-mail. Audit rows keep a supplier's IBAN as its last four, an inbox sender by the domain only, and an Airwallex deposit by its id, amount, currency and status. Data already synced keeps what it had (the chains are append-only).

### Purser Cloud
- **One cash figure:** the Ledger, Cash & FX, the Overview and Ask Purser show the same bank balance per currency.
- **One policy:** Cash & FX shows the floors and FX threshold of the company's own CFO policy.
- **Nederlands, complete:** with Dutch chosen, no English shows on any page, invoice, agent card or in Ask Purser (names, codes and amounts aside). English stays the main language.

## Upgrade notes
- Nothing to run by hand. The runner adds the trigger `one_live_transfer_per_bill` on open. A demo company made before this release is rebuilt on the next guest sign-in.
- **Behaviour change:** payments approved alone to one payee are totalled over `ap.split_days` (default 27 days); a payment covered by a CFO-signed task mandate counts against the mandate's own signed total instead.
