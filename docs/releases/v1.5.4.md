## Purser v1.5.4

**AI agents pay supplier invoices within the limits you set. Above a limit, the CFO signs the exact terms with a passkey (Face ID), and one changed cent breaks the signature.**

Test money only: the Airwallex sandbox and the Alta (formerly Metal) testnet. No real money moves; what is simulated is labelled in each record.

## Fixed in v1.5.4
- **The engine's approvals, fuzzed.** A seeded property test drives Purser Cloud's engine through random sequences (propose, approve with any key, reject, expiry, the emergency stop, a lost bank answer and retry, negotiation) and checks after every step that nothing is paid without the approvals its terms need, nothing while the stop is on, nothing twice, and always the exact signed amount. It found one bug, now fixed with a regression test: **four eyes counted one person's two keys, enrolled under two names, as two people.** Every approval now counts a person once, by their id.
- **Receivables:** the older matcher sends a payment reference that names two invoices to suspense for the CFO instead of settling the longer number, by the same rule as the new matcher.
- **Dutch console:** a task mandate's payee stays as synced; only a label after it is translated.
