## Purser v1.2.0

**AI agents pay supplier invoices. Above their limit, the CFO signs the exact terms with a passkey, and one changed cent breaks the signature.** Test money only: Airwallex sandbox, Alta (formerly Metal) testnet. No real money moves; what is simulated is labelled as such in each record.

### Proven live (sandbox and testnet, each with its run record)
- **Public /try:** 25 recorded journeys. 50 of 50 Airwallex sandbox transfers PAID, and 25 of 25 one-cent changes refused before anything was created. Claude reads every invoice live, and a visible notice appears if it ever falls back.
- **Airwallex:** 60 of 61 endpoints called live. Soak test: 30 of 30 payments read back as signed, 6 of 6 FX conversions. Spend Bills: 3 of 3.
- **Alta (formerly Metal) testnet:** 21 payments inside an agent's mandate, 3 attempts outside it refused on chain, and the anchor matches.
- **Browsers:** /try passes all 21 steps in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit (Safari's engine).

### Attacked and broken on purpose
- **Red team v2** on the /try API: 128 of 128 attacks stopped.
- **Chaos testing** of money movement: 317 scenarios, 77 process crashes and 492 injected faults, with **0 invariants broken**. Nothing was paid twice or without a signature, and every planted canary was caught.
- **Fuzzing:** 17 of 17 properties held over 315,300 runs.
- **Tamper matrix:** every signed field of every committed receipt and proof bundle was changed by one step, and every forged copy was refused.
- **Formal model** (TLA+): TLC checked 1,102,845 states with no error. Three planted bugs were each caught with a trace.

### AI under control
- **Held-out adversarial evals:** each case set was committed before its first run.
  - v3: 11 wrong payments with Purser vs 74 with no rules.
  - v4: 11 vs 72.
  - After the fixes, both are at 0 wrong payments and 0 false holds. The held-out results are kept as recorded.
- **New checks:**
  - split invoices;
  - near copies on the same day;
  - bank-change and frozen-account wording in 7 languages;
  - payment links;
  - Unicode, hex, ROT13 and base64 injection;
  - out-of-band channels (WhatsApp and similar);
  - line items that don't add up to the total.
- **New alarm:** an approved payment whose terms change after signing now raises a critical `/health` alarm, and it is never paid. Our own chaos test found this gap; it is closed in this release.

### Verifiable and auditable
- Every release is **signed with Sigstore** and carries SLSA build provenance, a CycloneDX SBOM and SHA256SUMS. Check it with `gh attestation verify <file> -R agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents`.
- The proof-bundle verifier is hardened. It never crashes on malformed input, and it states what a bundle file cannot prove.
- **Repository and security practices:**
  - OpenSSF Best Practices: passing.
  - CI checks: Scorecard, OSV-Scanner with VEX, Harden-Runner, REUSE, CodeQL, Semgrep and gitleaks.
  - Docs: a STRIDE threat model, a security mapping to NIST SSDF, OWASP LLM Top 10 and DORA (mapped, not certified), and GOVERNANCE.md with a continuity plan.
- **Quality:** 7,671 tests passing (6 skipped, 685 files), Lighthouse accessibility 100 on all public pages, and mutation testing on the proof verifier (50 of 51 planted bugs caught).
