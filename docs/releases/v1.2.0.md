## Purser v1.2.0

**AI agents pay supplier invoices. Above their limit, the CFO signs the exact terms with a passkey, and one changed cent breaks the signature.**

Test money only: the Airwallex sandbox and the Alta (formerly Metal) testnet. No real money moves; what is simulated is labelled in each record.

**Try it:** [/try](https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/try) · **Every figure with its record:** [/proof](https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/proof) · **Previous releases:** [v1.1-submission](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/releases/tag/v1.1-submission), [v1.0-submission](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/releases/tag/v1.0-submission)

### Attacked and broken on purpose
- **Red team v2 on the /try API:** all 128 attacks held. [Report](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evidence/redteam-api.md)
- **Chaos testing of money movement:** 317 scenarios, 77 process crashes, 492 injected faults, **0 money invariants broken**. Nothing was paid twice or without a signature. [Report](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evidence/chaos-money.md)
- **Fuzzing:** all 17 properties held over 315,300 runs. [Report](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evidence/fuzzing.md)
- **Tamper matrix:** every signed value of 6 proofs changed one at a time; all 894 forged copies refused, the originals verify. [Report](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evidence/tamper-matrix.md)
- **Formal model (TLA+):** TLC checked 1,102,845 states with no error; 3 planted bugs were each caught with a trace. [Model](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/formal/PurserPayment.tla) · [Report](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evidence/formal-model.md)
- **Mutation testing** on the proof verifier: 50 of 51 planted bugs caught (98%). [Report](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evidence/mutation-testing.md)

### AI under control
- **Held-out adversarial evals**, each case set committed before its first run:
  - v3: 11 wrong payments with Purser vs 74 with no rules. [Eval](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evals/adversarial-v3.md)
  - v4: 11 vs 72. [Eval](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evals/adversarial-v4.md)
  - After the fixes, both are at 0 wrong payments and 0 false holds. The held-out results stay as recorded.
- **New checks:**
  - split invoices and near copies on the same day;
  - bank-change and frozen-account wording in 7 languages;
  - payment links;
  - Unicode, hex, ROT13 and base64 injection;
  - out-of-band channels (WhatsApp and similar);
  - line items that don't add up to the total.
- **New alarm:** an approved payment whose terms change after signing raises a critical `/health` alarm and is never paid. Our own chaos test found this gap; it is closed in this release.

### Verifiable and auditable
- **Signed releases:** from this release on, the source archive, the CycloneDX SBOM and `SHA256SUMS` carry SLSA build provenance signed with Sigstore. Verify a download:
  ```
  gh attestation verify x402-business-agents-v1.2.0.tar.gz -R agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents
  ```
