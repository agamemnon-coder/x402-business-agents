## Purser v1.5.1

**AI agents pay supplier invoices within the limits you set. Above a limit, the CFO signs the exact terms with a passkey (Face ID), and one changed cent breaks the signature.**

Test money only: the Airwallex sandbox and the Alta (formerly Metal) testnet. No real money moves; what is simulated is labelled in each record.

### Fixed after an independent review of v1.5.0
- **Audit pack and compliance evidence:** never VALID without a pinned trusted key; the evidence and its audit pack must have one signer.
- **FX conversions:** the CFO's floor is judged on free money under the payout lock, the signed maximum is held, and the stop, pause and agent identity are checked again right before the create. A replaced payout re-checks its holds before its create too.
- **Receivables matching:** a reference names an invoice only as a whole word (NW-1077 no longer pays NW-10); an unknown number goes to review.
- **Segregation of duties:** above the threshold a policy change needs a proven proposer, who can never approve it.
- **Negotiation:** a deal that fails halfway is withdrawn, never left payable twice; the signed offer chain is checked before the deal is filed, and the agreement and offers are kept with the proof.
- **Cards in Purser Cloud:** refused while the company's emergency stop is on, also when the stop lives on its runner.

## Upgrade notes
- **Breaking for scripts:** `npm run verify-audit-pack` and `npm run verify-compliance-evidence` exit 3 (UNPINNED or PARTIAL) unless you pass `--trust <public key>` and, for evidence, `--audit-pack`.
- **Additive schema:** Purser Cloud adds `money_loops` (`cloud/migrations/0014_money_loops.sql`); the runner pushes its treasury and receivables snapshot with `npm run cloud -- push`.
- With `approval.policy_sod_eur` set, `npm run policy`, `entities` and `mandates` need `--by` for a loosening above it.
