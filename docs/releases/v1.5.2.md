## Purser v1.5.2

**AI agents pay supplier invoices within the limits you set. Above a limit, the CFO signs the exact terms with a passkey (Face ID), and one changed cent breaks the signature.**

Test money only: the Airwallex sandbox and the Alta (formerly Metal) testnet. No real money moves; what is simulated is labelled in each record.

## Fixed in v1.5.2

### The demo company's receivables agree everywhere
- The sales invoices on the Receivables page (EUR 7,702.41, GBP 980.00 and USD 1,450.00 open) are now booked in the demo company's own ledger by the runner's receivables code. The ledger's receivables report, the balance sheet and Ask Purser's cash position show the same figures; before, they showed nothing open.
- The customer payments matched to them appear in Transactions and the ledger, named by invoice number only. The demo's net cash is EUR 14,808.34, and Cash & FX reads the same figure.
- **On every runner:** sales invoices, receipts and write-offs are booked under the invoice number, so no customer name reaches Purser Cloud through the ledger. Payments the receivables module matched now show as money in on Transactions.

### Purser Cloud
- Due and overdue count from the demo's own last day (12 Nov), so invoices due later in November no longer read as overdue.

- A guest's language choice in Settings holds (Dutch works for the demo).
- An agent whose card rests frozen between purchases is no longer shown as paused.
- The Overview's tile says it counts invoices waiting for the CFO; "1 duplicate".
