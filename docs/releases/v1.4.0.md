## Purser v1.4.0

**AI agents pay supplier invoices within the limits you set. Above a limit, the CFO signs the exact terms with a passkey (Face ID), and one changed cent breaks the signature.**

Test money only: the Airwallex sandbox and the Alta (formerly Metal) testnet. No real money moves; what is simulated is labelled in each record.

**Try it:** [/try](https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/try) · **Every figure with its record:** [/proof](https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/proof) · **Security:** [/security](https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/security) · **Purser Cloud:** [purser-try.max-31b.workers.dev](https://purser-try.max-31b.workers.dev) · **Previous release:** [v1.3.2](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/releases/tag/v1.3.2)

### The hands-on demo (/try)
- **One demo card on the site's wave**, with three key numbers, each linked to its proof.
- **A new visitor starts on Bergtex Textil GmbH, BTX-2026-21, EUR 1,850.00.** Give it to the AI agent: above the agent's EUR 500.00 limit, so "CFO approval required". The EUR 120.00 invoice the agent pays alone is right under it as its own example.
- **The one-cent test reads "Payment refused. Nothing was paid."** After the CFO signs, asking EUR 1,850.01 is refused before any rail is called. Checked from a fresh browser session, and pinned in CI's judge check.
- **No account needed:** a sandbox company with test money, deleted after 24 hours.

### Proof and security
- **CFO policy as OPA/Rego.** `policy/purser.rego` restates Purser's three pure decisions rule for rule. CI runs 23,459 cases through a pinned, SHA-256-checked OPA v1.22.0 and requires the same outcome and reason as Purser on every one ([docs/policy-opa.md](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/policy-opa.md), with what is not exported). Purser's enforcement code is unchanged; OPA never decides a real payment.
- **/proof by category**, with "Under load" tiles read from their records: 10 visitors on /try at once, engine answer times, Alta clearing throughput, Airwallex sandbox create to PAID, 128 concurrent payments in CI.
- **/security in the new design**: 53 of 53 attacks on the payment path stopped, 66 of 66 one-cent changes refused after approval, 128 of 128 attacks on the /try API held, 172 Dafny proof obligations verified; each figure links to its record.

## Upgrade and security notes
- **No database migration** and no change to an API, MCP tool, CLI command, approval-terms schema or proof bundle.
- **/security's content security policy** allows one script, the wave, by its SHA-256 hash; nothing else.
- **CI** gains one step: a pinned OPA binary, checked against its SHA-256 before it runs.
- **Fuzzing**: the full set was run for this release; the report is in [docs/evidence/fuzzing.md](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evidence/fuzzing.md).
- **No vulnerabilities fixed** in this release.
