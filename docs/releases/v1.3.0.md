## Purser v1.3.0

**AI agents pay supplier invoices within the limits you set. Above a limit, the CFO signs the exact terms with a passkey (Face ID), and one changed cent breaks the signature.**

Test money only: the Airwallex sandbox and the Alta (formerly Metal) testnet. No real money moves; what is simulated is labelled in each record.

**Watch the demo (3:15):** [youtu.be/gEUm8RlhZac](https://youtu.be/gEUm8RlhZac) · **Try it:** [/try](https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/try) · **Every figure with its record:** [/proof](https://kit11.max-31b.workers.dev/proof) · **Previous release:** [v1.2.0](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/releases/tag/v1.2.0)

## New in v1.3.0

### Proven live (sandbox and testnet, 19 and 20 November)
- **The public /try, a mega run of 50 walks as a visitor**, in Chromium (1440, 768 and 390 px) and Firefox: every step the site was asked to do passed in 49 of 50 walks. [Record](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/runs/2026-11-19-mega-run.md)
- **Airwallex soak, 20 November:** 30 of 30 payments PAID with the signed hash read back and matching, 6 of 6 FX conversions, and 5 of 5 one-cent changes refused before any transfer was created (0 calls between the change and the refusal). [Record](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/runs/2026-11-20-airwallex-soak.md)
- **Counted on /proof:** 1,620 Claude calls on record, 264 Alta testnet transactions, 580 Airwallex sandbox API calls in 14 records, 33 live runs, each linked to its record.

### Every figure leads to its record
- Every figure, count, hash and transaction id on /proof links to the committed record it comes from, and a test fails when one does not. [Proof index](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evidence/proof-index.md)
- /status says its hourly-check numbers are live, and links them to /status.json.

### Connect it to your own systems
- Purser Cloud's "Your connections" lists every way a company can connect and whether each one is connected, not connected, or not yet available. Each company connects its own: Xero, QuickBooks Online and Exact Online, a Slack or Teams channel, sign-in with Google, Microsoft or Apple. Most of these are **built, not tested live** against the real service, and each one is labelled that way. No connection can approve a payment. [Connections](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/connections.md) · [Owner setup](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/integrations/owner-setup.md)

### Security
- **OWASP ASVS 4.0.3 Level 2 self-assessment:** 190 Pass, 22 Fail and 47 N/A of 259 requirements, each verdict with where it can be checked. A self-assessment, not an audit. [Assessment](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/security/asvs-l2.md)
- **Threat model on every pull request:** a pull request that changes security-sensitive code must say what it could let someone do and what stops it (ASVS V1.1.2).
- **Keys and sessions in Purser Cloud** (built and tested, on the test deployment once its database migrations run): a new approval key while another is active needs a second signature and cools off 72 hours; an owner's recovery needs two platform admins; a person can see and end their sessions.

### Quality
- **8,845 tests passing** (5 skipped, 814 files) on the last full run; coverage floor 90% statements, 79% branches.
- **OpenSSF Scorecard 7.3**, read on 20 November. [Record](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/evidence/scorecard.json)
- **The site:** the demo video is the main video everywhere, and the site's Worker starts about four times faster (from ~570 ms to ~150 ms).
