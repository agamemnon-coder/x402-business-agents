## Purser v1.5.0

**AI agents pay supplier invoices within the limits you set. Above a limit, the CFO signs the exact terms with a passkey (Face ID), and one changed cent breaks the signature.**

Test money only: the Airwallex sandbox and the Alta (formerly Metal) testnet. No real money moves; what is simulated is labelled in each record.

## New in v1.5.0: the governance round (21 to 26 November)

Read against Airwallex's "Agentic banking starts with governance": [docs/governance.md](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/governance.md) maps each principle to the code that holds it, the test that proves it and the live record, and says plainly what is not done.

### Governance
- **The CFO's emergency stop in Purser Cloud.** A red button in Settings (or `POST /v1/engine/emergency-stop`) files the stop as a policy change; it takes effect only when the CFO approves it with the passkey, and lifting it is signed the same way. While on, a red banner shows on every console page; nothing is planned, no agent pays alone, an approval pays nothing, the routed rail asks once more right before every create at either bank, and no agent card is issued, unfrozen or raised. **Run live on the Airwallex sandbox** for payments: a signed stop held a supplier payout at the rail with no create sent, and the lift took the passkey (12 of 12 steps, [record](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/runs/2026-11-26-stop-at-rail-live.md)). Freezing every card on a stop is tested against stand-ins.
- **Several legal entities, each paying only from its own account.** The terms the CFO signs carry the paying entity and its funding account, so changing either breaks the signature like one changed cent. An agent pays from an entity only where the CFO signed `pay_from.<entity>.<agent>` allowed; `npm run entities` lists, adds and allows them. **Run live** with two sandbox accounts: paid from entity A's own account, refused before any call from B's (12 of 12 steps, [record](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/runs/2026-11-26-entities-live.md)).
- **Card hours, metadata and controls in Airwallex Issuing.** Each agent card carries its agent and policy version in Airwallex's metadata; outside the hours the CFO set, Purser refuses a purchase before any call and the sync freezes the card at Airwallex (10 of 10 steps live, [record](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/runs/2026-11-26-card-governance-live.md)).
- **A failed payout replaced once** (behind `KIT11_REPLACE_FAILED_PAYOUT`): exactly one replacement under a request id derived from the first, never twice (10 of 10 steps live, [record](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/runs/2026-11-26-replace-failed-live.md)).
- **Policy simulator.** Before the CFO signs a policy change, `npm run policy -- simulate` replays the period's supplier payments under the policy in force and the proposed one with Purser's own decision code, and says per payment what would have changed. Read-only; shown on the dashboard and on the phone beside the waiting change, never part of the signed terms.
- **Approval notifications to Slack or Teams.** What waits for the CFO is posted to an incoming webhook with one link to the passkey page; nothing in a message approves. Off unless a webhook is set; tested with a fake endpoint only, nothing posted live.
- **Know your agent.** Each agent principal signs its money requests with its own Ed25519 key (time window, single-use nonce); the agent API checks it and the money actions record the key id. `PURSER_AGENT_IDENTITY=required|optional|off`.
- **The authority map in Purser Cloud.** What each agent may do, and who signed it, is pushed by the runner and shown under each card on the Agents page (`GET /v1/authority-map`).
- **Full task mandates in Cloud, task tokens and the payment's rationale.** The Cloud engine enforces task mandates like the runner; the autopilot pays on a short-lived task token (only its hash stored); each invoice page in Cloud says why it was paid this way.
- **Segregation of duties for policy changes.** Above `approval.policy_sod_eur` (off until set), loosening the CFO policy needs the passkeys of two different people; whoever proposed a change never approves it. Tightening stays one person; the phone says "Turns off segregation of duties" when a change would.
- **A signed audit pack per period.** `npm run audit-pack -- --from … --to …` writes one bundle: the period's audit rows with their hash chain back to genesis, sorted into decisions, approvals, policy changes, refusals and holds, and reconciliation, with an Ed25519-signed manifest. `npm run verify-audit-pack` checks it offline and names the section and row that fail.
- **Airwallex AgentOS alongside Purser.** AgentOS reads and prepares but does not initiate transfers, payouts or FX. Purser's MCP server gains `route_money_out`, a read-only tool that finds the checked invoice a transfer would pay and the next step; a guessed money tool name is refused and pointed at it. [docs/agentos.md](https://github.com/agamemnon-coder/x402-business-agents/blob/main/docs/agentos.md). Not an official integration.

## Upgrade and security notes
- **Additive schema only, nothing to run by hand.** The runner adds its new columns and tables on open (card hours, the policy version of a card transaction, agents' public keys, task-token hashes); Purser Cloud creates `authority_maps` on start (also `cloud/migrations/0012_authority_maps.sql` for an older database). Companies without entities hash and behave exactly as before; segregation of duties is off until the CFO sets its threshold.
- **New:** the Cloud routes `POST /v1/engine/emergency-stop` and `GET /v1/authority-map` (OpenAPI updated), the MCP tool `route_money_out`, and the CLIs `audit-pack`, `verify-audit-pack`, `entities`, `policy -- simulate` and `airwallex:governance-live`.
- **New settings, all off or optional by default:** `PURSER_NOTIFY_SLACK_WEBHOOK`, `PURSER_NOTIFY_TEAMS_WEBHOOK`, `PURSER_AGENT_IDENTITY`, `KIT11_TASK_TOKEN`, `KIT11_REPLACE_FAILED_PAYOUT`.
- **Fuzzing**: the nightly proof-bundle suite was rerun on three seeds; all 17 properties held.
- **Vulnerabilities fixed**: the three CodeQL findings and the `wcmatch` advisory above.
