# Purser: the video script (2:53)

Read it over the silent master (`master.mp4`). Each beat starts at the time shown; the subtitles (`subtitles.srt`) carry the same words.
The words run at about 134 a minute: speak calmly, and take a breath at each heading. Record each beat on its own if that is easier; the clips have the same names.

## 0:00 Hook (12 s, clip `01-open.mp4`)

AI agents are starting to spend company money: software, suppliers, refunds. So every CFO asks the same question: who is holding the pen?

## 0:12 The problem (12 s, clip `02-problem.mp4`)

Give an agent a card number and hope, or approve every cent yourself. One is a risk no auditor signs off. The other saves you nothing.

## 0:24 Purser (14 s, clip `03-reveal.mp4`)

Purser gives every AI agent its own company card and its own on-chain mandate, with limits the CFO sets once. Anything above those limits waits for the CFO's Face ID.

## 0:38 A company card, and Face ID (22 s, clip `04-card.mp4`)

Watch. The operations agent buys software: approved by Airwallex. Then the Pro plan, forty-five euros, on a thirty euro limit: declined, by Airwallex itself. The agent asks for more. The CFO checks the exact terms and approves with Face ID. The limit moves to fifty, and the same purchase goes through.

## 1:00 A euro bill, paid (22 s, clip `05-bill.mp4`)

A euro invoice arrives: eighteen hundred and fifty. The finance agent checks the reserve, takes a live quote and converts only the missing three hundred and seventy. One Face ID covers the rate, the payee and the most the fee may be. Airwallex sends it by SWIFT; in the sandbox it is marked paid.

## 1:22 On chain with Metal (30 s, clip `06-metal.mp4`)

On Metal, agents pay per request, cents at a time, under mandates that live on chain. When the supplier doubles its price, the payment hits the mandate's cap and bounces: nothing moves, and the customer is not charged. Purser prices two options, Opus reviews them, the CFO signs one, and the mandates are replaced on chain. Mandates, x402, signed orders and payouts ran here; clearing and a receive policy in separate live runs.

## 1:52 The Dispute desk (20 s, clip `07-dispute.mp4`)

Then a customer says: our agent never ordered this, refund everything. Northwind's Dispute desk checks the proof: every order signed by their agent, every payment inside their mandate on chain, every delivery matched. Twelve of twelve. Claim rejected; nothing more refunded.

## 2:12 Ask Purser (14 s, clip `08-ask.mp4`)

Need something once? Ask Purser: a flight to Berlin, at most one hundred and fifty euros. Airwallex issues a single-use card. The booking goes through; a second charge is declined.

## 2:26 Why you can trust it (17 s, clip `09-trust.mp4`)

Code decides; the models only read and advise. Thirty-nine red-team attacks, four by Claude Opus: all blocked. Eighteen scenarios: as expected. Optional four eyes, one Face ID to stop every agent and card, and about nine cents of AI per thousand euros.

## 2:43 Purser (10 s, clip `10-end.mp4`)

Purser. Agents move at machine speed, and the CFO keeps the pen. Every payment has a proof.
