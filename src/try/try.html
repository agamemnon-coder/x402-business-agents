What is real here. The models are real: Claude Haiku sorts the request and Claude Sonnet reads it, and you see what each call cost. The policy is Northwind's, with the limits from the recorded live runs (€50 a software purchase, task cards up to €300 without Face ID, every bank transfer with Face ID); on this page a copy of those rules is checked in code. What is simulated: the cards and the payments. Nothing on this page calls Airwallex or Metal, and no money exists here; prices without an amount come from a small demo catalog. In the live system the same decisions issue real Airwallex cards and pay on Metal, and every approval is the CFO's Face ID. Fair use: 6 tries a minute and 40 a day per visitor, 280 characters each; when many people try at once, or the day's budget for model calls is used, code alone reads the request and the page says so.
