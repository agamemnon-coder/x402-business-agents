How the CFO signs in

There is no password and no account to break into. Two things open Purser, and both stay on the CFO's own devices.

1. A private link to look

The runner on the company's own machine prints a viewer link. Its key sits after the #, so the browser keeps it and never sends it to a server. Open it once on the laptop or the phone. A new relay secret gives a new link and locks out every old one.

2. Face ID to decide

Approving needs the CFO's passkey: Face ID or Touch ID on the phone, bound to this site. It signs the exact terms (amount, payee, fee), and the runner checks that signature against the key it pinned when the CFO enrolled. A copied link can look, but never approve.

3. Enrolment you confirm

A new passkey only counts after the CFO reads its fingerprint off the phone and confirms it on the runner. A second approver for large amounts is added the same way.

You are a guest, so there is nothing to sign in to here. Everything the CFO sees is in the recorded CFO dashboard, and the demo walks through one real run step by step. To run it yourself: npm run judge.

Pay per request on Metal

For thousands of tiny payments, a limit the chain enforces.

Agents that buy data or API calls pay a few cents per request on Metal. Each pays under a mandate: a cap per payment, a total, one allowed payee and an end date. Ask for more than the mandate allows and the chain refuses; the CFO decides with the same Face ID.

Safe by design, not by prompt

Nothing an AI says can move money. Limits sit in the bank and on the chain; exceptions need you.

Face ID on the exact terms

You sign the precise amount, payee and fee. Change one character and the signature no longer fits.

Four eyes

Above your threshold, two different people approve. Whoever proposed it never can.

Emergency stop

One Face ID freezes every agent and every card at the bank.

Fakes and AI tricks blocked

Changed bank details and hidden "pre-approved" lines are caught before any money moves.

Speed limits, and our own red team

An agent that pays too often or too much is stopped by code. Our red team attacks Purser; every attack must fail.

A log you can check

Every payment, decline and approval in a tamper-evident log, re-checkable from public sources.
