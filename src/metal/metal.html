How Purser uses Metal

On Metal, Northwind's agents pay on chain, in cents, under mandates the chain itself enforces. Purser is the CFO's control layer on top: it sets the mandates, asks for Face ID before any limit goes up, and proves every payment afterwards. Everything below ran live on Metal testnet with test money; every card links to its transaction and to Metal's docs.
